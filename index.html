I am an MPhil student in Data Science and Analytics at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), advised by Prof. Zhijiang Guo and co-advised by Dr. Sibo Song (Qwen-VL Team, Alibaba). I will continue as a PhD student in the same group from 2027. I am currently a research intern at Huawei (Yinwang) 2030 Lab, working with Dr. Yinhong Liu and Prof. Qun Liu. Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Software Engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Xiaodong Gu.

My research has focused on multimodal reasoning and the internal reasoning mechanisms of LLMs. I have also contributed to several agentic RL projects. Currently, I am interested in building native multimodal foundation models that unify all modalities within a single model, and I am especially excited about Visual2Code.
